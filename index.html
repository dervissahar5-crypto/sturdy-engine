Asian Red Velvet Cake

  • Time:60minute
  • Serves:Cake pan, 3 layers high
Ingredients Quantity
yolkbatter: 3 large egg yolks
15g fine sugar
30ml vegetable oil
40ml milk
½ tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp Red Velvet flavor (or red food coloring)
57g cake flour (low protein)
meringue
3 large egg whites
A few drops of lemon juice (or vinegar)
40g fine sugar

Step 1:Prepare Yolk Batter: In a bowl, whisk egg yolks, 15g sugar, oil, milk, vanilla, and red velvet flavor until smooth. Sift in the cake flour and mix until well combined.
Step 2:Whip Meringue: In a clean bowl, whip egg whites and lemon juice until frothy. Gradually add the 40g sugar in three batches, whipping until medium-soft peaks form.
Step 3:Fold: Take ⅓ of the meringue and gently fold it into the yolk batter using a spatula. Pour this mixture back into the remaining meringue and fold gently until just combined. Do not overmix.
Step 4:Bake: Pour the batter into an ungreased 16cm or 18cm pan. Tap the pan once on the counter. Bake in a preheated oven at 150°C (300°F) for 35-40 minutes.
Step 5:Cool: Remove from the oven immediately, tap the pan once, and cool completely upside down before unmolding.
